Payment and scope

Payment. Your card is authorized when you request a consult and only charged when we accept — like a hold at a gas pump. If we decline, the authorization is released and no charge is made. After you submit this form you will be taken to our secure payment page.

Scope. A Rapid Consult is a base consult — up to 45 minutes plus a file review — a limited-scope conversation. It does not produce a written report, findings or an engineering opinion. If the matter converts to a full evaluation within 30 days, 50% of the consult fee is credited against it.

Conflicts. Valor runs a conflict check before scheduling. A conflict may mean we cannot take the consult; the appointment time you select is a request and is confirmed on acceptance.
